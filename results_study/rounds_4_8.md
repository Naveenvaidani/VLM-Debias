## 1. Pathway ablation (round 5)

After training, the visual pathway is switched off at test time and the test set is scored again. With the image off every model gives one answer for every input, so worst-group accuracy is 0 by construction and only average accuracy is informative: it falls to the majority-class share (77.8% on Waterbirds, 50% on Watermark) when the model has no image.
